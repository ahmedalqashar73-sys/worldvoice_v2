# A1 Podcast 01 — Meeting a New Friend

**Status:** Draft script for original human recording; NOT a playable episode. Do not publish until a licensed male and female recording has been completed and reviewed.

**Speakers:** Daniel (adult male), Anna (adult female). Use the same two performers in every WorldVoice Academy podcast episode.
**Target length:** 5:00, with a natural measured A1 delivery, short response pauses and brief pauses between ideas. Final timing must be checked on the actual recorded MP3.
**Editorial style:** conversational, friendly, natural interruptions and reactions, no third voice, no narration or background music required.

## English recording script

**Daniel:** Hi! Is this seat free? The café is very busy today.

**Anna:** Yes, it is. Please sit down. Are you waiting for someone?

**Daniel:** No, I am new here. I just want some coffee.

**Anna:** Welcome! My name is Anna. What is your name?

**Daniel:** I'm Daniel. Nice to meet you, Anna.

**Anna:** Nice to meet you too. Do you live near here?

**Daniel:** Yes, I live on Green Street. I moved here last week.

**Anna:** Oh, really? I live on that street too!

**Daniel:** Really? That is funny. Maybe we are neighbors.

**Anna:** Maybe! My home is next to a small bookshop.

**Daniel:** I know that bookshop. It has a blue door.

**Anna:** Yes, that's the one. Do you like books?

**Daniel:** I do. I read short stories before I go to bed.

**Anna:** Me too. I also listen to music in the evening.

**Daniel:** What kind of music do you like?

**Anna:** I like quiet music. What about you?

**Daniel:** I like jazz, but sometimes I listen to pop music.

**Anna:** That's nice. Oh, is your coffee coming?

**Daniel:** Not yet. I want a small coffee with milk.

**Anna:** Good choice. I usually have tea, but today I want coffee.

**Daniel:** Do you come to this café every day?

**Anna:** Not every day. I come on Tuesday and Friday.

**Daniel:** Why Tuesday and Friday? Are those your days off?

**Anna:** No. I work at the library near the park.

**Daniel:** A library? That sounds great! Do you like your job?

**Anna:** Yes, I love it. I meet many interesting people.

**Daniel:** I work at a small school. I'm an English teacher.

**Anna:** An English teacher! Do your students like speaking English?

**Daniel:** Some students love it. Other students feel shy at first.

**Anna:** I understand. I feel shy when I speak Spanish.

**Daniel:** You speak Spanish? I want to learn Spanish too.

**Anna:** Just a little! I know simple words and some questions.

**Daniel:** Maybe we can practice together one day.

**Anna:** That sounds fun. We can learn something new every week.

**Daniel:** Great idea. By the way, where is the park?

**Anna:** It's easy. Go straight, then turn left at the bakery.

**Daniel:** Go straight and turn left. Is it far from here?

**Anna:** No, only five minutes on foot. The trees are beautiful.

**Daniel:** I like walking. I usually walk after dinner.

**Anna:** I walk in the morning. I like the fresh air.

**Daniel:** What time do you start work at the library?

**Anna:** At nine. I leave home at eight thirty.

**Daniel:** That's a good time. My school starts at eight.

**Anna:** Eight? That's early! Do you eat breakfast at home?

**Daniel:** Yes, I have eggs and bread. And you?

**Anna:** I have fruit and yogurt. Sometimes I eat toast.

**Daniel:** Now I'm hungry. Maybe I need a sandwich too.

**Anna:** Ha! The sandwiches here are good. I like the cheese one.

**Daniel:** Okay, I'll try it. Thank you for the idea.

**Anna:** You're welcome. Look, your coffee is here now.

**Daniel:** Finally! It smells nice. Would you like to sit longer?

**Anna:** I'd love to, but I need to go to work soon.

**Daniel:** No problem. Maybe we can meet here on Friday?

**Anna:** Friday is good for me. How about ten o'clock?

**Daniel:** Ten o'clock sounds perfect. We can talk about our week.

**Anna:** And we can practice a little Spanish, if you like.

**Daniel:** Yes, please. But you may need to help me a lot!

**Anna:** That's okay. We can make mistakes and learn together.

**Daniel:** I like that. I'm happy I sat here today.

**Anna:** Me too, Daniel. Welcome to the neighborhood!

**Daniel:** Thank you, Anna. See you on Friday.

**Anna:** See you on Friday. Enjoy your coffee and your sandwich!
